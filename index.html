Publications

ShapDBM: Exploring decision boundary maps in shapley space

Authors: Luke Watkin, Daniel Archambault, Alex Telea

Venue: EuroVis 2026, Short Papers

Summary: This paper presents a new technique for computing Decision Boundary Maps (DBMs) for complex datasets by computing the base projection using the Shapley values of our chosen dataset. We find our technique produce visualisations of similar or higher quality compared to data-only derived DBMs.

GD Contest (2025) - Visualisation of Netflix's 'Dark'

Authors: Tom Baumgartl, Luke Watkin, George Bell

Venue: GD 2026 Contest

Summary: This project was conducted as part of an international graph drawing contest for GD 2025, where the task was to produce a visualisation of Netflix's Dark TV show, a German science fiction show with non-linear storylines and time travel. A particular challenge of this contest was to utilise a 360-degree screen at the Nörrkoping Decision Arena. Our team consisted of an international collaboration between Newcastle University and the University of Cologne. The final visualisation was a spiral storyline that used the screen to emphasize the time loop aspect of the narrative. Of the contestants, we placed 3rd overall.
