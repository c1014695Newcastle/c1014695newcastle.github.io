Publications

ShapDBM: Exploring decision boundary maps in shapley space

Authors: Luke Watkin, Daniel Archambault, Alex Telea

Venue: EuroVis 2026, Short Papers

Summary: This paper presents a new technique for computing Decision Boundary Maps (DBMs) for complex datasets by computing the base projection using the Shapley values of our chosen dataset. We find our technique produce visualisations of similar or higher quality compared to data-only derived DBMs.
